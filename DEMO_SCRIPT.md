# 3-minute demo (all names and centres are synthetic)
- **0:00–0:20 Hook.** "On 4 October a 19-year-old in Panna died after the health centre had no anti-venom (TOI). Families waste the golden hour driving centre to centre."
- **0:20–1:00 Family (phone, Hindi).** Meera (synthetic) scans a QR at a bus stand and taps साँप ने काटा. Demo PHC Rampur-1 shows 🔴, Demo CHC Sonpur 🟢 (12 vials, updated 40 min ago). She taps Directions.
- **1:00–1:30 Report.** At Rampur-1 she taps "यहाँ नहीं मिला" → "Out of stock". She gets a receipt and a proof link. No login, no wallet.
- **1:30–2:05 Staff.** Demo nurse at Rampur-1 posts ASV 0 → later 8 vials. She opens the inbox and taps Confirm. Show the extension in the explorer.
- **2:05–2:35 Dashboard and proof.** Public, keyless: centres out of stock, stale >6h, unacknowledged reports. Open one record: creator wallet, expiry, readonly flag. "Nobody, not even us, can edit it."
- **2:35–3:00 Why Arkiv.** Expiring stock (6h) = no stale lies. `$creator` = only the centre can post. Readonly reports = the citizen's record can't be erased. Open source (MIT).
