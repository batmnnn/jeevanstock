# JeevanStock graphic prompts (v9)

Shared rules for every asset. These are UI layers, not posters.

- One subject group, centered, with clear space around it.
- Flat pure magenta background, hex #FF00FF. No gradient, no floor, no cast shadow, no border.
- No letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, or Hindi.
- No people, no blood, no striking fangs, no open wounds.
- Style: refined gouache illustration, soft studio light, gentle volume, no thick outlines.
- Palette: teal #0F766E, cobalt #1D4ED8, rose #BE123C, cream glass, warm highlights.
- Magenta must not appear inside the subject.

## 1. vials — hero layer

A small still life of three glass medicine vials standing together. The left vial holds teal serum, the middle one cobalt, the right one rose. Cream rubber stoppers, thin glass highlights, a short cotton roll beside them. Three-quarter product view. Calm, precise, like a pharmacy tray cropped out of a Figma health flow. Square frame.

## 2. snake — snakebite choice

One teal glass vial of antivenom beside a small, calm spectacled cobra drawn as a medical emblem, coiled and still, head in profile, not striking. The snake is the same gouache language as the vial. Both objects form one group. Square frame.

## 3. dog — dog-bite choice

One cobalt glass vaccine vial beside a calm seated Indian pariah dog, ears soft, mouth closed, no snarl. The dog is small enough to sit next to the vial as one group. Square frame.

## 4. clinic — centre list

A single-storey Indian primary health centre: whitewashed walls, a teal door, a small rose cross on the wall with no lettering, a neem tree at one side, warm daylight. The building is the only subject. Square frame.

## 5. sky — back layer

A wide morning sky only. No ground, no buildings. The lower edge is a soft periwinkle #EEF2FF, rising into a pale cool blue. A few large cream clouds, gouache, quiet, no sun disk, no birds, no text. This layer is opaque. 16:9.

## 6. hills — far layer

A long low band of distant farmland ridges in dusty sage and soft blue, sitting in the lower half of the frame. The upper half is flat magenta #FF00FF so the hills can be cut out. No buildings, no people, no text. 16:9.

## 7. trees — side layer

Two neem trees with soft green canopies and visible trunks, standing together, gouache, same light as the clinic. Flat magenta #FF00FF background, no ground plane. 3:4.

## 8. grass — front layer

A low strip of grass and small shrubs along the bottom edge only. The top two thirds of the frame is flat magenta #FF00FF. No path text, no people. 16:9.
